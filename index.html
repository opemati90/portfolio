About

About

I am a Senior Product Designer with 7+ years designing B2B and consumer products across climate technology, fintech, digital assets, healthcare and logistics.

Where I do my strongest work

Complex products where users need to understand dense data, complete regulated workflows, or make high-impact decisions. I work across product discovery, user research, interaction design, information architecture, design systems, prototyping and usability testing.

How I use AI tooling

I use AI-assisted development tools to turn detailed requirements and designs into functional prototypes. That lets a team test real product behaviour and find problems before implementation gets expensive. The responsibility I hold is product design, not software engineering.
