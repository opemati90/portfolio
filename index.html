About

A designer who can read code, run a sprint, and ship.

Senior product designer and AI design engineer in Berlin, seven years in. My work lives on platforms heavy with data and rules: fintech, climate, health, and AI, in-house and at agencies. I design the system, prototype it in code, and ship, most recently Anstoss, an Android app I built solo.

How I work

Research comes first. I run interviews and usability tests before I open Figma. Then I prototype in code, not just Figma, using AI build tools like Claude Code and Cursor to reach a working build fast. I stay with engineers through the build, because a handoff doc has never shipped a screen on its own.

What sets me apart

I design and I build. I've owned an entire enterprise platform solo, shipped my own Android app end to end, and designed AI features where model confidence and human oversight were the real problem. I write production code, understand cloud infrastructure, and hold Scrum, Product Owner, and Google Cloud Security certifications.
