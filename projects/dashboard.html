Framing the Problem

5W1H: Defining the design challenge

Who

SaaS founders, operations managers, and growth leads checking metrics daily

What

A unified dashboard combining user metrics, revenue, projects, and traffic data

When

Every morning (10-second check) and weekly (deep-dive analysis sessions)

Where

Web application, desktop-first, used as the first tab opened each morning

Why

Founders check 5+ tools daily. Average 45 minutes compiling a business snapshot. No unified view exists

How

KPI cards with trend indicators, time-series charts, calendar widget, traffic breakdown, and user management table
